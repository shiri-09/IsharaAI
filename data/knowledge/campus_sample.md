# Sample Campus Guide (demo data)

This is SAMPLE data for demonstrating Ishara AI. Replace these files in data/knowledge/ with your institution's real information. Every answer the assistant gives from this file names it as the source.

## Computer Science department office
The Computer Science (CSE) department office is on the 2nd floor of Block A, room A-204. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM. The lift in Block A reaches the 2nd floor. Students can meet the Head of Department by appointment on weekdays between 10 AM and 1 PM.

## Data Science department office
The Data Science department office is on the 3rd floor of Block A, room A-305. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM. Faculty meeting hours for students are 2 PM to 4 PM on weekdays.

## Booking an appointment with a professor
Appointments with professors are 15-minute slots on weekdays between 10 AM and 1 PM, or 2 PM and 4 PM. Book at least one day in advance. Bring your student ID. You can ask Ishara AI to book an appointment; it will ask you to confirm before booking.

## Accessible routes and lifts
Block A and Block B both have lifts near the main entrance. Ramps are available at the main gate, the library entrance and the canteen. The path from the main gate to Block A is step-free. Block C has no lift; classes for students who need step-free access can be moved to Block A or B on request at the department office.

## Accessible washrooms
Accessible washrooms are on the ground floor of Block A (next to the lift), the ground floor of Block B, and inside the library on the ground floor.

## Sign language support desk
An Indian Sign Language interpreter is available at the Student Support desk, ground floor of Block B, Monday to Friday from 10 AM to 3 PM. Students can request an interpreter for a class or meeting with one day's notice.

## Library
The library is in Block B, ground and first floor. It is open from 8 AM to 8 PM on weekdays and 9 AM to 2 PM on Saturdays. It has a quiet study room, two computers with screen readers, and a step-free entrance with a ramp.

## Canteen
The canteen is next to Block B and is open from 8 AM to 6 PM. It has a step-free entrance and visual token display boards, so orders can be collected without listening for a name.

## Medical room
The medical room is on the ground floor of Block A, room A-002. A nurse is available from 9 AM to 5 PM on weekdays. For emergencies, call 112 (India's emergency number) and inform campus security at the main gate.

## Exams and results
Internal assessments are held in the 8th and 14th weeks of the semester. Students who need extra time or a sign language interpreter for exams should apply at the Student Support desk two weeks before the exam.
